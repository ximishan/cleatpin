# Cleatpin lore and player-facing copy

Authoritative for tone and for English strings the player reads. Mechanics live in [RULES.md](RULES.md). Visuals live in [ART.md](ART.md).

Tone is light maritime folklore, cartoon, PEGI 12. The dock is a workshop with a superstition, told like a story the bosun already knows. People are adult workers. Enemy “boats” are boats, with a painted bow mark, and they come apart into fittings. Speech is short, dry, and kind.

Cleatpin is its own game. It is separate from Pollen Pocket. It is a salvage dock, with pins, rope, salt, tar, and brass.

## Story

The dock’s old rule: wreckage brought ashore must be pinned into the cleats so the wrecks can come to rest. Someone hauled up a tug that should have stayed down and broke it wrong. The bargain broke, and the tide keeps sending those ships back.

You are Bosun Reed. You pin what you stop. The wall is built from what you just halted.

The Wreck-tug is that wrongly broken tug. It appears at the end of Fog Wharf and Open Bay. Stopping it drops scrap pegs, the same as any stubborn wreck, and it does not leave a new kind of prize.

Squall is a harsher weather clear of the same map. Deadcalm is the ominous still before the storm. Endless Tide is the same cove after the schedule runs out, and the water does not turn.

Backup names from early sketches — Lantern Cordon, Lampwright — are not places in this story and are not modes.

## In-game intro

Shown on the dock-rule card. Three sentences, no more:

> The dock’s old rule was simple: wreckage brought ashore must be pinned into the cleats, so the wrecks can come to rest. Someone hauled up a tug that should have stayed down and broke it wrong; the bargain broke, and the tide keeps sending those ships back. You are Bosun Reed, and you pin what you stop.

Title logline, under the wordmark:

> Pin what you stop. The wall is built from what you just halted.

## People

**Bosun Reed** (v1, the only hero on the boards). Short, broad, calm, fussy about a proper seat. They carry a boathook in two hands and wear a knit cap, never a captain’s hat. They do not do a pirate voice.

**Mara Quay** (v1 flavor). The dock clerk. Tall, quiet, counts everything, never scolds. Appears as a portrait on the victory and defeat cards only.

**Pip** (v1 flavor). A gull on the title cleat and on the locker. Does not talk.

**Lighter-hand Nia Pell** (v3, after all six campaign maps are cleared). Quick, cheerful, talks in dock calls. Hood up, hitching pole, two hands. One hero stands on the boards at a time. Reed stays the default.

| Name | Line |
| --- | --- |
